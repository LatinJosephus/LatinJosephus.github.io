Our editions and translations

Latin Contra Apionem

The Latin Contra Apionem presented here uses Karl Boysen’s edition of 1898. R.M. Pollard prepared the electronic edition in 2026.

Source and references

Boysen’s Flavii Iosephi opera ex versione Latina antiqua. Pars VI: De Iudaeorum vetustate sive Contra Apionem libri II was published as CSEL 37 (Vienna, Prague and Leipzig, 1898).

View Boysen’s printed edition · Read the Contra Apionem.

The two books use Niese section references: I.1–320 and II.1–296. Chapter divisions and Niese sections are represented separately in the reader.

English translations

The reader offers Whiston and Lodge. Their shared discussion pages also cover the Antiquities and the Jewish War.
