Bellum witnesses

The Bellum Judaicum reader offers Cardwell (1837) in Latin, Niese in Greek, and two independently selectable English translations: Whiston, translated from Greek, and Thomas Lodge (1602), whose title page describes his translation as “translated out of the Latin, and French”.

Lodge’s text comes from EEBO-TCP A04680, released under CC0 1.0. LatinJosephus supplies its structural correspondence with the site’s Cardwell chapters and 704 coarse units, together with the independently accepted 4,001-section Niese alignment. Lodge’s original wording and TCP source markup are preserved.

Fuller discussion: Latin Bellum, Whiston and Lodge.

Lodge Antiquities Proem and Books I–XX

The Antiquities reader also offers Thomas Lodge’s Proem and Books I–XX from The famous and memorable workes of Iosephus (London, 1602), transcribed by EEBO-TCP as A04680. The underlying TCP transcription is released under CC0 1.0. LatinJosephus independently supplies the certified Niese segmentation of its 7,376 sections, preserving Lodge’s wording, printed chapters and source evidence. This source licence concerns the TCP transcription; it does not assign a licence to the project’s independent scholarly apparatus. The Proem is independently accessible as I.1–26; Book I narrative begins at I.27. The complete Antiquities is available in this reader.

Fuller discussion: Lodge’s Antiquities.

Lodge Contra Apionem Books I–II

The Contra Apionem reader offers Thomas Lodge’s two books from The famous and memorable workes of Iosephus (London, 1602), using EEBO-TCP A04680. The original heading describes the work as “WRITTEN AGAINST APION, AS TOVCHING THE ANTIQVITIE OF THE IEWES.” The underlying TCP transcription is released under CC0 1.0 Universal. LatinJosephus independently aligns all 616 Niese sections, retaining the original headings, paragraphs, marginal notes, gaps and source order. Six historically MEDIUM boundaries were explicitly human approved; their interwoven or paraphrastic correspondences remain source specific. The TCP licence does not assign a licence to this independent segmentation apparatus.

Fuller discussion: Lodge’s Contra Apionem.
